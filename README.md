The KSR (Korps Sukarela) website is designed to serve as an information and communication platform for members of the Red Cross Volunteer Corps and the general public. With a clean and accessible layout, the website provides updates on humanitarian activities, training schedules, emergency response efforts, and organizational announcements.

Key sections include:

About KSR – introducing the vision, mission, and structure of the organization.

Activities – highlighting recent and upcoming volunteer events, blood donation drives, and disaster response efforts.

Membership Info – providing guidance on how to join KSR, requirements, and benefits of being a volunteer.

Gallery – showcasing photos and videos of past events and activities.

Contact & Social Media Links – enabling visitors to connect directly with the team.

The website plays an important role in promoting volunteerism, strengthening community outreach, and supporting the mission of the Indonesian Red Cross.
